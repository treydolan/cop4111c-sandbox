Flexbox

One of the ways you can choose to organize your layouts is using the flexbox properties.

Flex Direction

flex(-TYP)(-DIR)

TYP
  • row
  • col
DIR

reverse

Example:

  • 1 2 3 4
    flex-row
  • 1 2 3 4
    flex-row-reverse
  • 1 2 3 4
    flex-col
  • 1 2 3 4
    flex-col-reverse

Flex Wrap

flex-wrap

flex-wrap-reverse

flex-nowrap

You can tell an item to wrap to a next line or not with these classes. The default is for items not to wrap, but you can also have them wrap in reverse.

Example:

  • 1 2 3 4
    flex-wrap
  • 1 2 3 4
    flex-wrap-reverse
  • 1 2 3 4
    flex-nowrap

Flex

  • flex-1: lets an item grow and shrink as needed, but ignores the original size.
  • flex-auto: lets an item grow and shrink as needed, taking into account the original size.
  • flex-initial: lets an item shrink, but not grow.
  • flex-none: prevents an item from growing or shrinking.

Example:

  • 1 2 3
    flex-initial
  • 1 2 3
    flex-1
  • 1 2 3
    flex-auto
  • 1 2 3
    flex-none

Flex Grow

flex-grow-0 lets an item grow to fit the available space.

flex-code prevents and item from growning.

Example:

  • 1 2 3
    flex-grow
  • 1 2 3
    flex-grow-0

Flex Shrink

flex-shrink-0prevents an item from shrinking

flex-shrinkallows an item to shrink if needed.

Example:

  • 1 2 3
    flex-shrink
  • 1 2 3
    flex-shrink-0
