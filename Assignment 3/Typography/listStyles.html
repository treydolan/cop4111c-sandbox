List Styles

by default, lists are shown without bullet points or numbers and without any indenting, so these styles allow you to control those parameters and pretty much insert them back.

List Style Type

list-

TYP: none, disc, decimal

Example:

  • Favorite Rivets
    • Buck rivets
    • Olympic rivets
    • Pop rivets
    list-style-type: none;
    list-none
  • Favorite Rivets
    • Buck rivets
    • Olympic rivets
    • Pop rivets
    list-style-type: disc;
    list-disc
  • Favorite Rivets
    • Buck rivets
    • Olympic rivets
    • Pop rivets
    list-style-type: decimal;
    list-decimal

List Style Position

list-

TYP
  • inside
  • outside

Example:

  • Favorite Rivets
    • Buck rivets
    • Olympic rivets
    • Pop rivets
    list-style-position: outside;
    list-outside
  • Favorite Rivets
    • Buck rivets
    • Olympic rivets
    • Pop rivets
    list-style-position: inside;
    list-inside
