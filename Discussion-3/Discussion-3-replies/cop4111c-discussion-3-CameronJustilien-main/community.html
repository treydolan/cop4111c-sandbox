Our Community

In our company, we always are willing to listen to our community. We constantly find ways to understand what our users want from our services, and attempt to make an effort to improve at every angle. We don't only want to make a profit, but to make our users happy.

Frequently asked Questions

  • Where are the courses?

    We do not have the courses ready right now, but it is in our best interest to make sure that they will be useful and engaging for our users when we add them to our website.

  • Will the courses be interactive?

    Yes, we do plan on making our users code in the courses so they know how it's like to code. There will be challenges, games and more.

  • Will the courses cost money?

    Yes, most of the courses will need to be paid to take them, but there will be few basic courses that will be free. These courses will only have reading material and a few videos to learn from. So, our users do need to pay to benefit from our services.
